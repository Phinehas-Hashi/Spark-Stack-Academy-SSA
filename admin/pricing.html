Central pricing configuration

These settings are stored centrally and changed only through a trusted server function. Institution onboarding fees use Daraja STK Push once credentials are configured. Recurring subscription checkout, invoices and automated renewals remain a separate billing milestone.

01 · ONBOARDING

Institution onboarding fee

One-time setup charge for newly approved institutions.

Onboarding checkout currently uses Safaricom M-Pesa Daraja and requires a KES amount. Payment prompts are sent to the institution contact phone supplied during registration.

02 · SUBSCRIPTIONS

Institution subscription pricing

Configure the recurring base price for institution workspaces.

03 · PLATFORM FEES

Institution transaction fee

Optional platform fee applied to eligible institution transactions.

The transaction fee remains disabled by default until formally approved. Direct institution settlement remains the configured settlement model.
