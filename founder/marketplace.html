03 · COMMERCIAL GOVERNANCE

Marketplace transaction principles

  • One marketplace catalogue; product records identify their type, owner, source, price, currency and publication state.
  • Course learning content remains in the existing courses collection; the marketplace item references that course rather than duplicating its lessons.
  • Instructor course revenue follows the approved 75% instructor / 25% Academy split.
  • Institution course revenue uses the institution's approved direct-settlement arrangement; any platform transaction fee remains separately configurable.
  • Mentorship, professional services, bundles, boot camps, digital products, add-ons and corporate training require their own fulfilment and refund rules before checkout is enabled.
  • Student memberships and institution subscriptions are recurring products and need billing, renewal, cancellation and invoice workflows before launch.
